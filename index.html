About Our School

A place where learning meets opportunity.

Shree Siddhartha Memorial Academy (H.S.) is a private educational institution located in Sainamaina-8, Ramapur, Rupandehi, Nepal. The school provides education from Nursery to Grade 10 and focuses on academic learning, discipline, creativity, skills and the overall development of students.


Our goal is to provide students with a supportive learning environment where they can develop knowledge, confidence, responsibility and a positive attitude toward their future.
