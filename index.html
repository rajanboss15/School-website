About Our School

Learning • Discipline • Creativity • Excellence

Shree Siddhartha Memorial Academy (H.S.) is a private school located at Sainamaina-8, Ramapur, Rupandehi, Nepal. The school provides education from Nursery to Grade 10 and focuses on quality education, academic development, discipline, sports and creative activities.


With dedicated teachers and a supportive learning environment, the school encourages students to develop knowledge, confidence, creativity and responsibility.
